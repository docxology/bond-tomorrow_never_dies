# Conclusion

CARVER demonstrates that the media-warfare instruments of *Tomorrow Never
Dies* admit quantitative, reproducible models. Disinformation spreads through
media exposure amplified by social diffusion; a stealth hull trades radar
cross-section for a halving of detection range under the fourth-root law; and
a single GPS jammer degrades navigation accuracy and cascades failure through
the dependency web of a fleet. Each result is deterministic and tested
without mocks, and every number in this manuscript is injected from the
canonical CARVER scenario rather than hand-authored.

Because the core is exposed through the BOND-API `MissionProvider` protocol,
the same analysis is available as a discoverable mission that a coordinator
can brief, plan, execute, and debrief — with provenance that lets any outcome
be audited against its inputs.
